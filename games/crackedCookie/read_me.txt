This game was originally part of a game called Rhythm Nights with Tappa, a game jam game made for the Freddit Game Jam.
This minigame is the third minigame in the game, and is the only Rhythm Heaven minigame, official or not, to my knowledge to be in 6/8 time (Donk-Donk is in 4/4 with triplets fight me).

Cracked Cookie takes place in the cookie corner of the Tappa Pizzeria with Poly Parrot on a higher ledge and YOU standing underneath holding oversized knives.
Poly makes a screech when dropping a cookie, and depending on the type of screech, YOU has to cut either the left side or the right side.
Cutting left uses + and cutting right uses A. In the original, left used Space and right used the Arrow Keys.
