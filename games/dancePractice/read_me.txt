This game was originally part of a game called Rhythm Nights with Tappa, a game jam game made for the Freddit Game Jam.
This minigame is the fourth in the game, and is a keep-the-beat-type game.

Dance Practice takes place backstage with Tappa Tapper and YOU joining him.
Tappa makes a pattern of squeaks, and each measure after follows that pattern, needing YOU to tap with him using A/+.
There is an additional cue to stop tapping, with the second sound playing on the same beat as the last tap.

In the original game, there are only three patterns; one of four quarter notes, one of two half notes, and one of three dotted quarter notes.
Other patterns can be used, and I suggest that you do to fit the beat, but following some rules:
- Patterns are a measure long.
- A pattern's last cue is the second squeak.
