This game was originally part of a game called Rhythm Nights with Tappa, a game jam game made for the Freddit Game Jam.
This minigame is the first actual minigame in RNwT, and is also the simplist, kinda.

Pizza Slicer takes place inside the Tappa Pizzeria, in the dining room because past me thought that was a good idea.
Samurai Jane throws pizza ingredients at YOU, who has a large pizza cutter. There are three cues: cheese, olive, and pepperoni.
Cheese has one cut as it only has one syllable, olive has two cuts as it has two syllables, and pepperoni has three slices because syllables had nothing to do with the amount of cuts.
Press A to cut.
