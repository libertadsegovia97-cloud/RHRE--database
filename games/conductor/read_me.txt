This game was originally part of a game called Rhythm Nights with Tappa, a game jam game made for the Freddit Game Jam.
This minigame is the second game in RNwT, and also my least favorite.

Conductor takes place on the main stage, with Cass the Conductor conducting YOU on the tenor drum.
Cass says, "me," then a pattern of "up," "down," "left," or "right." Cass then says, "you," then expects YOU to do the same on the drums.
The inputs use + in whatever direction Cass said.
